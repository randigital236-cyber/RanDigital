Frequently Asked Questions

What is Ran Digital (RND)?
Ran Digital is a BEP-20 utility token on BNB Smart Chain focused on blockchain education, digital services, and community-driven ecosystem with fixed supply of 20,000 tokens.
Is there a transaction tax?
No. RND has 0% buy tax and 0% sell tax, making it ideal for trading and long-term holding.
How is liquidity secured?
Liquidity is locked for 2+ years via trusted locker and ownership is renounced, ensuring full community safety.
Where can I buy RND?
RND is available on PancakeSwap (BNB Smart Chain). Use the contract address from this site.
What is the total supply?
Fixed supply of 20,000 RND. No minting, no inflation.
