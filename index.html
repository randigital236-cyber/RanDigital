Frequently Asked Questions

What makes RND suitable for CEX listing?
RND project information and contract details are published for review. Exchange listing decisions are made independently, and no listing approval is guaranteed.
Is the contract verified and audited?
Check the contract verification status on BscScan. Do not treat contract verification as a security audit; publish an audit claim only when a verifiable report is available.
Where can I buy RND?
RND is available on PancakeSwap (BNB Smart Chain). Use the contract address above.
What is the total supply and tax structure?
Total supply is fixed at 20,000 RND. There is 0% tax on buys and 0% on sells — no hidden fees.
Is the team doxxed / KYC'd?
The project does not publicly disclose individual team identities. Do not interpret this privacy choice as a claim that team KYC has been completed.
